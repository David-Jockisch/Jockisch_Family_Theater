PART I
FOUNDATION
===============================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE

The Jockisch Family Theater (JFT) Movie Guide exists to provide consistent,
professional, family-oriented evaluations of films within the Jockisch Family
Theater collection.

Unlike commercial review sites, the JFT Movie Guide is not intended to judge
whether a film is "good" or "bad" based on popularity, critical reception,
financial success, or awards. It is also not intended to persuade viewers to
adopt a particular opinion of a film.

Instead, every guide exists to answer two completely independent questions.

Question 1

How good is this movie as a home theater experience?

Question 2

Is there anything a Christian family should know before choosing to watch it?

These questions intentionally remain independent throughout every review.

The technical quality of a film is represented by the JFT Score.

Family awareness is represented by the Biblical Discernment section.

Neither category influences the other.

===============================================================================

1.2 MISSION STATEMENT

The mission of the Jockisch Family Theater Movie Guide is to celebrate
exceptional filmmaking while equipping families with factual, balanced
information that helps them make informed viewing decisions.

The guide values craftsmanship, artistic excellence, immersive home theater
experiences, and honest discernment without sensationalism or unnecessary
criticism.

Every review should encourage appreciation for filmmaking while respecting that
every family applies biblical convictions differently.

===============================================================================

1.3 CORE PRINCIPLES

The following principles govern every review written under the JFT standard.

Principle One

Technical excellence is evaluated independently from biblical discernment.

A technically outstanding movie should receive the score it deserves regardless
of its worldview or content.

Likewise, a wholesome family movie should not receive a higher score simply
because its message aligns with Christian values.

The JFT Score measures presentation—not morality.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle Two

Biblical Discernment exists to inform, not condemn.

The Biblical Discernment section provides objective information that allows
parents and families to make their own decisions.

Its purpose is awareness.

It is never intended to shame viewers or assign spiritual value to a film.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle Three

Accuracy is more important than opinion.

Every statement should be truthful, balanced, and supported by the content of
the film whenever possible.

Avoid exaggeration.

Avoid minimizing.

Describe rather than persuade.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle Four

Consistency builds trust.

Every movie should be evaluated using the same standards.

The popularity of a franchise...

The nostalgia attached to a film...

The opinions of critics...

The personal preferences of the reviewer...

None of these should significantly alter how a movie is scored.

If two films deliver nearly identical technical experiences, they should receive
similar scores regardless of genre or popularity.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle Five

Celebrate great filmmaking.

The JFT Movie Guide exists because movies are capable of remarkable artistic
achievement.

Great cinematography.

Beautiful music.

Exceptional performances.

Groundbreaking visual effects.

Immersive Dolby Atmos sound.

Reference-quality HDR.

Creative storytelling.

These qualities should be recognized and celebrated whenever they appear.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Principle Six

Maintain a hopeful perspective.

Even when discussing difficult content, reviews should remain respectful,
professional, and constructive.

The guide should never sound cynical, angry, sarcastic, or dismissive.

Instead, reviews should acknowledge both strengths and weaknesses with humility
and fairness.

===============================================================================

1.4 WHAT THE JFT MOVIE GUIDE IS NOT

The JFT Movie Guide is NOT:

• A replacement for IMDb.

• A replacement for Rotten Tomatoes.

• A replacement for Letterboxd.

• A replacement for professional film criticism.

• A theological review.

• A parental control system.

• A content filtering service.

Instead, it is a personalized editorial guide created specifically for the
Jockisch Family Theater that combines home theater evaluation with factual
family awareness.

===============================================================================

1.5 TARGET AUDIENCE

The JFT Movie Guide is written primarily for:

• The Jockisch family.

• Friends visiting the theater.

• Guests choosing a movie.

• Home theater enthusiasts.

• Christian families who appreciate objective content awareness.

The guide assumes readers enjoy movies and want to appreciate excellent
filmmaking while remaining informed about potentially objectionable content.

===============================================================================

1.6 LONG-TERM VISION

The long-term goal of the JFT Movie Guide is to become a complete editorial
library covering every movie owned by the Jockisch Family Theater.

Every guide should appear as though it was written by the same editor, following
the same philosophy, using the same terminology, and applying the same scoring
standards.

As the library grows beyond one hundred films, consistency becomes increasingly
important. Every future addition should integrate seamlessly with guides written
years earlier, ensuring that the collection remains cohesive and trustworthy.

This document serves as the authoritative reference for maintaining that
consistency.

PART II
GUIDE TYPES AND REVIEW STRUCTURE
===============================================================================

2.1 TWO TYPES OF JFT MOVIE GUIDES

Every movie in the Jockisch Family Theater collection shall receive one of two
guide types.

The guide type is determined solely by the highest-quality edition owned.

No exceptions.

This distinction exists because the JFT Score evaluates the complete home
theater experience. If the best presentation is not available in the collection,
the guide should avoid assigning a technical score that the theater cannot
currently represent.

===============================================================================

2.2 FULL JFT REVIEW

A Full JFT Review represents the definitive evaluation of a movie.

This guide is reserved for movies owned in their highest-quality consumer
presentation.

Examples include:

• 4K UHD Blu-ray
• Blu-ray when no official 4K release exists

The assumption is that the Jockisch Family Theater can reproduce the movie in
its intended premium home theater presentation.

A Full Review includes every section of the guide.

Required sections:

• Movie Information
• JFT Score
• Classification
• Tagline
• Why It Earned Its Score
• Theater Experience
• Biblical Discernment
• Best Edition
• Demo Scenes
• Final Thoughts

The Full Review represents the "complete" JFT evaluation.

===============================================================================

2.3 COLLECTION GUIDE

A Collection Guide exists for movies that are owned but cannot yet receive a
complete technical evaluation.

Examples include:

• Digital ownership
• DVD ownership
• Blu-ray ownership when a superior 4K edition exists

The purpose of a Collection Guide is to provide useful information while
acknowledging that the definitive home theater presentation has not yet been
added to the collection.

Collection Guides intentionally omit the technical scoring system.

Included sections:

• Movie Information
• Biblical Discernment
• Best Edition
• Final Thoughts

Omitted sections:

• JFT Score
• Classification
• Tagline
• Why It Earned Its Score
• Theater Experience
• Demo Scenes

Within the guide data structure these omitted sections shall be represented as:

rating: null

theater: null

demo: null

The Collection App is responsible for hiding these sections during rendering.

===============================================================================

2.4 FUTURE UPGRADES

Collection Guides are not permanent.

Whenever a movie is upgraded to its best available edition, the Collection Guide
shall be replaced by a Full JFT Review.

Examples:

Digital → 4K UHD Blu-ray

DVD → Blu-ray

Blu-ray → 4K UHD Blu-ray

The existing Biblical Discernment and Final Thoughts may be retained and refined
during the upgrade process.

A new technical evaluation shall then be completed.

===============================================================================

2.5 REVIEW TEMPLATE

Every Full Review follows the exact same order.

Movie Information

↓

JFT Score

↓

Classification

↓

Tagline

↓

Why It Earned Its Score

↓

Theater Experience

↓

Biblical Discernment

↓

Best Edition

↓

Demo Scenes

↓

Final Thoughts

This order shall never change.

Consistency in presentation allows readers to immediately locate information
without relearning the layout for each guide.

===============================================================================

2.6 COLLECTION GUIDE TEMPLATE

Collection Guides follow a simplified structure.

Movie Information

↓

Collection Guide Badge

↓

Biblical Discernment

↓

Best Edition

↓

Final Thoughts

The simplified format intentionally communicates that the movie is part of the
collection but has not yet received its definitive JFT Review.

===============================================================================

2.7 DATA STRUCTURE

Every guide shall use the same JavaScript structure regardless of guide type.

module.exports = {

    info: {},

    rating: null,

    theater: null,

    biblicalDiscernment: {},

    ownership: {},

    demo: null,

    finalThoughts: ""

};

Using a universal structure greatly simplifies rendering within the Collection
App while maintaining consistency between Full Reviews and Collection Guides.

Unused sections shall always be set to null.

Placeholder text such as "N/A" shall never be used.

===============================================================================

2.8 RENDERING PHILOSOPHY

The Collection App should never need to know whether a guide is a Full Review or
a Collection Guide.

Instead, it should simply render every section that contains data and hide every
section that is null.

This philosophy keeps the rendering engine simple while allowing future guide
types to be added without changing the application.

===============================================================================

2.9 FUTURE EXPANSION

The universal guide structure has been intentionally designed to allow future
sections to be added without redesigning existing reviews.

Possible future additions include:

• Awards
• Historical Significance
• Trivia
• Behind the Scenes
• Reference Theater Rankings
• Director Spotlight
• Franchise Timeline

Future sections should remain optional whenever possible to preserve backwards
compatibility with existing guides.

PART III
THE JFT SCORING STANDARD
===============================================================================

3.1 PURPOSE OF THE JFT SCORE

The JFT Score measures the technical presentation of a movie as experienced in
the Jockisch Family Theater.

It is not a review of the movie itself.

It is not a measure of storytelling quality.

It is not a recommendation based on personal enjoyment.

Instead, the JFT Score answers one question:

"How well does this movie demonstrate the capabilities of the Jockisch Family
Theater?"

The score exists to evaluate the home theater experience—not the film's artistic
or narrative merit.

===============================================================================

3.2 THE JFT SCORE IS A HOME THEATER PRESENTATION RATING

The JFT Score should be viewed as a technical benchmark.

A movie with an average story may receive an outstanding JFT Score if it offers
reference-quality picture and sound.

Likewise, a beloved classic may receive a modest JFT Score if its presentation
does not fully utilize the capabilities of a modern home theater.

The JFT Score intentionally differs from traditional movie ratings.

It is designed specifically for home theater enthusiasts who want to know how
well a film showcases their equipment.

===============================================================================

3.3 WHAT THE JFT SCORE MEASURES

Every score represents the overall technical presentation of the movie.

The reviewer should consider how effectively the film demonstrates the
capabilities of a high-quality home theater.

Primary considerations include:

• Picture Quality

• HDR Presentation

• Contrast

• Black Levels

• Shadow Detail

• Highlight Detail

• Color Accuracy

• Color Volume

• Sharpness

• Fine Detail

• Dolby Atmos Presentation

• Surround Activity

• Height Channel Usage

• Dialogue Clarity

• Dynamic Range

• Bass Performance

• Audio Fidelity

• Sound Design

• Visual Effects Presentation

• Demo Value

No individual category receives its own numerical value.

Instead, these elements combine to form an overall impression of the technical
experience.

===============================================================================

3.4 WHAT THE JFT SCORE DOES NOT MEASURE

The following subjects shall never influence the JFT Score.

• Story quality

• Screenplay

• Acting performances

• Character development

• Emotional impact

• Humor

• Genre preference

• Personal enjoyment

• Nostalgia

• Awards

• Critical reception

• Box office performance

• Franchise popularity

• Political viewpoints

• Biblical worldview

• Violence

• Language

• Sexual content

• Nudity

• Drug or alcohol use

• Personal agreement with the themes of the film

These subjects may be discussed elsewhere within the guide but shall never
increase or decrease the JFT Score.

===============================================================================

3.5 THE REVIEWER'S MINDSET

When assigning a score, the reviewer should imagine inviting someone into the
Jockisch Family Theater for the first time.

The question is not:

"Would I recommend this movie?"

Instead ask:

"Would this movie effectively demonstrate what this theater is capable of?"

A technically exceptional movie should receive the score it deserves regardless
of whether the reviewer enjoys the story.

Likewise, a favorite movie should not receive a higher score simply because it
holds sentimental value.

===============================================================================

3.6 DEMO VALUE

Demo Value is one of the most important considerations within the JFT Score.

Movies with high Demo Value immediately showcase the strengths of the theater.

Examples include:

• Spectacular HDR

• Powerful Dolby Atmos

• Active surround effects

• Deep, clean bass

• Stunning visual effects

• Exceptional image clarity

• Memorable reference scenes

Movies frequently used to demonstrate a home theater naturally trend toward the
highest JFT Scores.

===============================================================================

3.7 THE COMPLETE PRESENTATION

The JFT Score reflects the complete technical presentation rather than isolated
specifications.

For example:

Excellent HDR cannot fully compensate for weak audio.

Likewise, incredible Atmos cannot completely overcome a poor transfer.

The score should represent the entire presentation working together to create an
immersive home theater experience.

===============================================================================

3.8 CONSISTENCY OVER SUBJECTIVITY

Every movie should be evaluated using the same technical standards.

The reviewer should avoid allowing personal opinions to influence the score.

A movie should not receive additional points because:

• It is a favorite.

• It won awards.

• It has nostalgic value.

• It is critically acclaimed.

Likewise, a movie should not lose points because:

• The reviewer dislikes the story.

• The reviewer disagrees with its message.

• The reviewer dislikes the genre.

Consistency builds confidence in the guide and ensures meaningful comparisons
between every review.

===============================================================================

3.9 SCORE STABILITY

The JFT Score should remain stable over time.

Scores should only change when meaningful technical reasons exist.

Examples include:

• A superior 4K restoration.

• A remastered Dolby Atmos mix.

• Discovery of previously overlooked technical issues.

• Recalibration after comparison with established JFT reference titles.

Scores should never fluctuate simply because public opinion changes.

===============================================================================

3.10 THE JFT REFERENCE STANDARD

As the JFT library grows, previously reviewed films become calibration
benchmarks.

Every new review should be compared against established reference titles.

The reviewer should continually ask:

"Is this presentation better than Dune: Part Two?"

"Is this Atmos mix stronger than Top Gun: Maverick?"

"Does this HDR presentation exceed Blade Runner 2049?"

Using existing reviews as anchors produces a scoring system that remains
consistent across hundreds of movies.

The goal is not mathematical precision.

The goal is dependable consistency.

PART IV
TECHNICAL EVALUATION STANDARDS
===============================================================================

4.1 PURPOSE

The Technical Evaluation Standards define the qualities that contribute to the
JFT Score.

These categories exist to help the reviewer consistently evaluate a movie's
presentation.

They are not individually scored.

Instead, they form the foundation of the reviewer's overall impression of the
home theater experience.

===============================================================================

4.2 PICTURE QUALITY

Picture Quality evaluates the overall clarity and fidelity of the image.

Considerations include:

• Image sharpness
• Fine detail
• Compression artifacts
• Source quality
• Overall cleanliness

Questions to consider:

Does the image appear exceptionally detailed?

Does the transfer appear clean and free of distracting artifacts?

Does the presentation feel worthy of a premium home theater?

===============================================================================

4.3 HDR PRESENTATION

HDR Presentation evaluates how effectively High Dynamic Range enhances the
viewing experience.

Considerations include:

• Bright highlights
• Deep blacks
• Dynamic contrast
• Specular highlights
• Natural lighting
• HDR consistency

Questions to consider:

Does HDR create a noticeable improvement?

Do bright scenes feel vibrant without losing detail?

Do dark scenes preserve shadow detail while maintaining deep blacks?

===============================================================================

4.4 COLOR PRESENTATION

Color Presentation evaluates the richness, accuracy, and artistic use of color.

Considerations include:

• Natural skin tones
• Wide color gamut
• Saturation
• Color separation
• Artistic grading

Questions to consider:

Do colors appear lifelike?

Does the grading enhance the atmosphere?

Are colors vibrant without appearing exaggerated?

===============================================================================

4.5 CONTRAST AND DEPTH

Contrast evaluates the perceived dimensionality of the image.

Considerations include:

• Separation between light and dark
• Depth perception
• Black level performance
• Shadow definition

Questions to consider:

Does the image feel three-dimensional?

Do scenes maintain detail in both bright and dark areas?

===============================================================================

4.6 DOLBY ATMOS PRESENTATION

Atmos Presentation evaluates how effectively the soundtrack creates an
immersive sound field.

Considerations include:

• Surround activity
• Height channel usage
• Object movement
• Spatial accuracy
• Immersion

Questions to consider:

Does the soundtrack fully utilize the theater?

Are sounds placed naturally throughout the room?

Do overhead effects feel intentional rather than occasional?

===============================================================================

4.7 DIALOGUE CLARITY

Dialogue should remain intelligible throughout the movie.

Considerations include:

• Center channel clarity
• Vocal balance
• Consistency
• Natural tone

Questions to consider:

Can dialogue be understood without increasing the volume?

Does speech remain clear during loud action sequences?

===============================================================================

4.8 DYNAMIC RANGE

Dynamic Range evaluates the difference between the quietest and loudest
moments.

Considerations include:

• Quiet ambience
• Sudden impact
• Crescendos
• Overall realism

Questions to consider:

Does the soundtrack feel alive?

Do loud moments have genuine impact?

Do quiet scenes create tension rather than sounding flat?

===============================================================================

4.9 BASS PERFORMANCE

Bass Performance evaluates low-frequency impact and integration.

Considerations include:

• Extension
• Cleanliness
• Impact
• Control
• Integration

Questions to consider:

Does bass feel powerful without becoming overwhelming?

Does it support the scene rather than distract from it?

Are low frequencies tight and well controlled?

===============================================================================

4.10 SURROUND IMMERSION

Surround Immersion evaluates how convincingly the movie places the viewer
inside its environment.

Considerations include:

• Ambient effects
• Environmental realism
• Seamless transitions
• Room-filling presentation

Questions to consider:

Do you feel surrounded by the environment?

Does the room disappear during key scenes?

===============================================================================

4.11 VISUAL EFFECTS PRESENTATION

This category evaluates how convincingly visual effects integrate into the
presentation.

The emphasis is not on the quantity of effects, but on their quality.

Considerations include:

• Realism
• Integration
• Image stability
• Believability

Questions to consider:

Do effects appear convincing?

Do they enhance immersion rather than distract from it?

===============================================================================

4.12 DEMO VALUE

Demo Value reflects how often scenes from the movie would be selected to
demonstrate the capabilities of the Jockisch Family Theater.

Movies with exceptional Demo Value often contain memorable scenes featuring:

• Outstanding HDR

• Reference-quality Atmos

• Powerful bass

• Exceptional cinematography

• Immersive sound design

• Stunning visual moments

Demo Value is not determined by the quality of the story.

===============================================================================

4.13 OVERALL PRESENTATION

No movie excels equally in every category.

Some films feature remarkable HDR but conservative Atmos mixes.

Others deliver spectacular sound while offering modest visuals.

The reviewer should evaluate how successfully every technical element works
together to create a cohesive home theater experience.

The final JFT Score represents the complete presentation—not the average of
individual strengths and weaknesses.

===============================================================================

4.14 REVIEWER REMINDER

Throughout the evaluation process, remember the purpose of the JFT Score.

Do not ask:

"Did I enjoy this movie?"

Instead ask:

"Did this movie allow the Jockisch Family Theater to perform at its highest
potential?"

That question should guide every technical evaluation.

PART V
BIBLICAL DISCERNMENT STANDARD
===============================================================================

5.1 PURPOSE

The Biblical Discernment section exists to provide factual, balanced awareness
of content that Christian families may wish to consider before viewing a movie.

It is not intended to replace personal conviction.

It is not intended to condemn a film.

It is not intended to determine whether someone should or should not watch a
movie.

Instead, it provides information that allows every family to make informed
decisions according to their own biblical convictions.

The Biblical Discernment section never influences the JFT Score.

===============================================================================

5.2 PHILOSOPHY

The JFT Movie Guide recognizes that faithful Christians may reach different
conclusions regarding entertainment.

For this reason, Biblical Discernment should always be presented objectively.

The reviewer should describe content honestly without exaggeration,
sensationalism, or unnecessary detail.

The goal is awareness.

Not persuasion.

===============================================================================

5.3 WRITING STYLE

Biblical Discernment should always remain:

• Respectful

• Objective

• Factual

• Balanced

• Concise

Avoid emotionally charged language.

Avoid sarcasm.

Avoid ridicule.

Avoid speculation regarding the intentions of filmmakers.

State what appears in the film rather than assuming motives behind it.

===============================================================================

5.4 OVERVIEW

The Overview serves as a brief summary of the movie's overall content.

It should highlight the most significant areas families may wish to know before
watching the film.

The Overview should normally consist of one concise paragraph.

Example:

"This action film contains frequent combat violence, several strong language
occurrences, and occasional frightening scenes. While themes of sacrifice and
courage are present, parents should be aware of the film's intense action
throughout."

===============================================================================

5.5 VIOLENCE

Describe violence factually.

Consider:

• Frequency

• Intensity

• Realism

• Graphic detail

• Emotional impact

Examples of observations include:

Frequent action violence.

Fantasy combat.

Military warfare.

Gunfire.

Explosions.

Martial arts.

Blood.

Graphic injuries.

Torture.

Execution.

Avoid assigning moral judgments.

Describe what the viewer will encounter.

===============================================================================

5.6 LANGUAGE

Summarize notable language without attempting to document every occurrence.

Mention:

• Mild profanity

• Moderate profanity

• Strong profanity

• Repeated coarse language

When appropriate, mention if language is infrequent or pervasive.

===============================================================================

5.7 SEXUAL CONTENT

Describe romantic or sexual content objectively.

Possible observations include:

Flirting.

Romantic relationships.

Passionate kissing.

Sexual references.

Implied intimacy.

Sex scenes.

The emphasis should remain factual rather than graphic.

===============================================================================

5.8 NUDITY

If nudity is present, describe it factually.

Examples include:

Brief non-sexual nudity.

Partial nudity.

Extended nudity.

Non-explicit bathing scenes.

Explicit nudity.

Avoid unnecessary detail.

===============================================================================

5.9 SUBSTANCES

Describe the use of:

Alcohol.

Smoking.

Drugs.

Intoxication.

Drug abuse.

When appropriate, indicate whether the behavior is portrayed positively,
negatively, or simply exists within the story.

===============================================================================

5.10 LGBT INFLUENCE

This section exists to acknowledge notable LGBT-related content when present.

Possible observations include:

Background representation.

Supporting characters.

Primary characters.

Romantic relationships.

Marriage.

Dialogue promoting related viewpoints.

The reviewer should simply describe what appears within the film.

Avoid argumentative commentary.

Avoid speculation.

If no notable LGBT-related content exists, this section may be omitted.

===============================================================================

5.11 SPIRITUAL THEMES

Describe spiritual elements appearing within the movie.

Examples include:

Prayer.

Miracles.

Angels.

Demons.

Occult practices.

Magic.

Necromancy.

False religions.

Witchcraft.

Idolatry.

Ghosts.

Supernatural powers.

Biblical themes.

The goal is to inform families of spiritual subject matter rather than determine
whether it is acceptable.

===============================================================================

5.12 Biblical Virtues

Biblical Discernment should acknowledge positive virtues when they are clearly
present.

Examples include:

Sacrifice.

Forgiveness.

Compassion.

Faithfulness.

Courage.

Justice.

Humility.

Selflessness.

Hope.

Redemption.

Perseverance.

These observations should remain descriptive rather than preachy.

===============================================================================

5.13 FAMILY SUITABILITY

Rather than assigning age recommendations, briefly summarize what types of
viewers may find the content appropriate or challenging.

Example:

"Older teens and adults will likely handle the intensity well, while younger
children may find several sequences frightening."

Avoid presenting this section as an official parental rating.

===============================================================================

5.14 REVIEWER REMINDER

The Biblical Discernment section should always answer one question:

"What should a Christian family know before watching this movie?"

It should never answer:

"Should Christians watch this movie?"

That decision belongs to each individual and family under their own convictions.

PART VI
EDITORIAL WRITING STANDARDS
===============================================================================

6.1 PURPOSE

Every JFT Movie Guide should feel as though it was written by the same editor.

Regardless of when a review is written or who contributes to it, readers should
experience a consistent tone, vocabulary, structure, and level of detail.

The purpose of these standards is to maintain a unified editorial voice across
the entire Jockisch Family Theater library.

===============================================================================

6.2 EDITORIAL VOICE

The JFT editorial voice should always be:

• Professional

• Knowledgeable

• Warm

• Respectful

• Family-oriented

• Encouraging

• Objective

Reviews should demonstrate enthusiasm for home theater without becoming overly
technical or difficult to understand.

The goal is to inform and encourage rather than impress.

===============================================================================

6.3 TONE

Every guide should maintain a positive and balanced tone.

Even when discussing weaknesses, avoid sounding dismissive or cynical.

Instead of criticizing a movie, explain where its technical presentation falls
short.

Examples:

Preferred:

"The Atmos mix remains fairly restrained throughout much of the film."

Avoid:

"The Atmos mix is disappointing."

Preferred:

"HDR highlights are effective, though they are used sparingly."

Avoid:

"The HDR is weak."

Describe.

Do not dramatize.

===============================================================================

6.4 OBJECTIVITY

Editorial opinions should always be supported by observable presentation.

Avoid statements that cannot be reasonably defended.

Preferred:

"Surround activity is subtle for much of the film."

Avoid:

"The sound design is boring."

Preferred:

"Dialogue remains consistently clear, even during action sequences."

Avoid:

"The audio mix is perfect."

Use measurable observations whenever possible.

===============================================================================

6.5 CONSISTENCY OF TERMINOLOGY

Use consistent vocabulary throughout every review.

Preferred terminology includes:

Reference-quality

Immersive

Natural

Subtle

Dynamic

Detailed

Balanced

Controlled

Refined

Clean

Crisp

Punchy

Layered

Atmospheric

Avoid constantly inventing new ways to describe the same concepts.

Consistency improves readability.

===============================================================================

6.6 AVOID HYPERBOLE

Do not exaggerate.

Avoid phrases such as:

"Absolutely insane."

"Mind-blowing."

"The greatest ever."

"Unbelievable."

"The best Atmos mix in history."

Instead, use professional language.

Example:

"The Atmos presentation ranks among the strongest currently available on disc."

===============================================================================

6.7 WRITE FOR THE READER

Assume the reader enjoys movies but may not understand advanced home theater
terminology.

Explain technical concepts naturally.

Instead of listing specifications, describe the viewing experience.

Example:

Preferred:

"Rain falls convincingly around the listening position, creating a convincing
sense of space."

Avoid:

"The soundtrack demonstrates aggressive object-based panning utilizing the
height array."

The guide should remain approachable for casual viewers.

===============================================================================

6.8 BE SPECIFIC

Whenever possible, describe what the viewer will actually experience.

Preferred:

"Explosions produce deep, room-filling bass with excellent control."

Avoid:

"The bass is good."

Preferred:

"Bright reflections on metal surfaces demonstrate excellent HDR."

Avoid:

"The HDR looks nice."

Specific observations create more useful reviews.

===============================================================================

6.9 AVOID SPOILERS

Movie Guides should remain largely spoiler-free.

When discussing demo scenes or technical presentation, describe the sequence
without revealing important plot developments.

Preferred:

"A nighttime chase sequence demonstrates exceptional surround immersion."

Avoid:

"The climactic battle after the main character..."

Protect the viewing experience whenever possible.

===============================================================================

6.10 BALANCE

Every review should acknowledge both strengths and weaknesses.

Very few movies excel equally in every technical category.

Likewise, very few movies fail completely.

Balanced reviews build credibility.

===============================================================================

6.11 FINAL THOUGHTS

The Final Thoughts section serves as the editorial conclusion.

It should summarize:

• The movie's technical presentation.

• Who would appreciate it.

• Whether it belongs in a serious home theater collection.

Avoid repeating the numerical score.

Instead, reinforce the overall impression left by the presentation.

===============================================================================

6.12 EDITORIAL INTEGRITY

Never adjust a score to match:

IMDb ratings.

Rotten Tomatoes scores.

Public opinion.

Online rankings.

Awards.

Personal nostalgia.

The JFT Guide exists to evaluate the home theater presentation independently.

Readers should trust that every score reflects the same editorial philosophy.

===============================================================================

6.13 THE REVIEWER'S CHECKLIST

Before publishing a guide, ask:

✓ Does this review follow the JFT philosophy?

✓ Is the score based only on technical presentation?

✓ Is Biblical Discernment separate from the score?

✓ Is every statement factual and balanced?

✓ Have unnecessary spoilers been avoided?

✓ Does the guide maintain the JFT editorial voice?

✓ Would this review feel at home beside every other JFT guide?

If every answer is yes, the review is ready for publication.

PART VII
RESEARCH & VERIFICATION STANDARDS
===============================================================================

7.1 PURPOSE

Every JFT Movie Guide should be built upon a foundation of accurate research,
careful observation, and honest evaluation.

Research exists to provide context and technical information before viewing a
film.

The final JFT Score, however, must always be based upon the movie's actual
performance within the Jockisch Family Theater.

The goal of research is to inform the reviewer—not replace the reviewer's own
experience.

7.5 PERSONAL EVALUATION

Research concludes when playback begins.

within the Jockisch Family Theater.

Observe the movie carefully and evaluate the complete experience.

Pay particular attention to:

• Picture quality

• HDR performance

• Color reproduction

• Contrast and black levels

• Dolby Atmos presentation

• Surround immersion

• Dialogue clarity

• Dynamic range

• Bass performance

• Demo-worthy sequences

7.6 VERIFY — DO NOT COPY

The JFT Movie Guide is an independent editorial publication.

Outside reviews should never determine a JFT Score or influence the wording of a
review.

If multiple reviewers praise or criticize a particular aspect of a movie,
attempt to verify those observations personally.

Agreement should come through observation—not repetition.

The following should never influence a JFT Score:

• IMDb ratings

• Rotten Tomatoes

• Metacritic

• Letterboxd

• Professional review scores

• Award nominations

• Award wins

• Box office performance

• Online rankings

The JFT Score exists independently from every other rating system.

7.8 WHEN EXPERTS DISAGREE

Technical reviewers occasionally reach different conclusions regarding a video
transfer or audio presentation.

When disagreements occur:

Research multiple respected sources.

View the movie personally.

Determine which observations best match the presentation experienced within the
Jockisch Family Theater.

Reasonable disagreement is acceptable.

Independent evaluation is encouraged.

===============================================================================

7.9 EDITORIAL INTEGRITY

Every published review should represent an honest technical evaluation.

Never inflate a score because a movie is popular.

Never lower a score because of personal preference.

Never attempt to align a review with public expectations.

Readers should trust that every JFT Score reflects the same editorial standards
and review philosophy.

The Jockisch Family Theater is the final authority for every review.

Research informs.

Observation confirms.

The purpose of every JFT Movie Guide is to document how a movie performs within
the Jockisch Family Theater—not how it performs in someone else's system.

Every review should ultimately answer one question:

"How well did this movie showcase the capabilities of the Jockisch Family
Theater?"

That answer forms the foundation of every JFT Score.

PART VIII
JFT REFERENCE LIBRARY & SCORE CALIBRATION
===============================================================================

8.1 PURPOSE

The purpose of the JFT Reference Library is to establish a stable foundation for
the JFT Score.

Rather than relying solely upon written descriptions of numerical scores, the
JFT Score is calibrated against established reference presentations.

Every new review should be compared against previously evaluated reference
titles to maintain long-term consistency.

===============================================================================

8.2 WHY CALIBRATION MATTERS

Without reference points, scoring naturally drifts over time.

A movie that feels like a 9.5 today may feel like a 9.2 after several years of
watching superior presentations.

Likewise, an exceptional transfer may receive an inflated score if reviewed
before experiencing true reference-quality material.

The Reference Library prevents score inflation and ensures meaningful
comparisons across the entire JFT collection.

===============================================================================

8.3 THE CALIBRATION QUESTION

When assigning a JFT Score, avoid asking:

"What number feels right?"

Instead ask:

"How does this presentation compare to the established JFT reference titles?"

The answer should naturally guide the score.

===============================================================================

8.4 REFERENCE TITLES

Reference titles represent benchmark presentations in one or more technical
categories.

They are not necessarily the highest-scoring movies.

Instead, they define what excellence looks like in specific areas.

Examples may include:

• HDR performance

• Dolby Atmos presentation

• Bass performance

• Contrast

• Shadow detail

• Color reproduction

• Surround immersion

• Overall presentation

Reference titles may evolve as superior releases become available.

8.6 COMPARATIVE SCORING

Every new review should be mentally compared against existing reference titles.

Example questions include:

"Does this HDR presentation surpass Dune: Part Two?"

"Is the Atmos mix more immersive than Top Gun: Maverick?"

"Does the contrast exceed Blade Runner 2049?"

"Is the environmental immersion comparable to 1917?"

These comparisons naturally lead toward a consistent score.

===============================================================================

8.7 SCORE ADJUSTMENTS

Occasionally, previously reviewed movies may require recalibration.

Reasons may include:

• Discovery of superior reference material

• Improved technical understanding

• New restoration releases

• Updated Atmos mixes

• Significant theater upgrades

When adjustments occur, they should be applied consistently across the library.

===============================================================================

8.8 REFERENCE TITLES ARE NOT IMMUTABLE

The Reference Library is intended to evolve.

As new films push the boundaries of home theater presentation, they may become
new calibration standards.

Older references may be replaced when a clearly superior presentation becomes
available.

The goal is continuous improvement while maintaining historical consistency.

===============================================================================

8.9 SCORE DISTRIBUTION

Perfect scores should remain exceptionally rare.

Likewise, extremely poor scores should be uncommon.

Most technically competent releases will naturally fall within the middle of
the scale.

Only presentations demonstrating truly exceptional technical achievement should
occupy the highest tier.

This approach preserves the significance of elite scores.

===============================================================================

8.10 THE REVIEWER'S REMINDER

The purpose of the Reference Library is consistency.

Do not attempt to force every movie into a predetermined score.

Instead, compare the presentation honestly against the established reference
titles.

The JFT Score should reflect where the movie belongs within the library—not
where it ranks among public opinion.

Every review strengthens the Reference Library.

Every reference title strengthens the JFT Score.

PART IX
PRESENTATION CLASSIFICATIONS
===============================================================================

9.1 PURPOSE

While the JFT Score provides precise numerical evaluation, Presentation
Classifications provide readers with an immediate understanding of a movie's
overall technical quality.

The classification complements the score but never replaces it.

Every Full JFT Review shall include both a numerical JFT Score and a
Presentation Classification.

===============================================================================

9.2 CLASSIFICATION PHILOSOPHY

Presentation Classifications exist to translate technical scores into meaningful
descriptions.

Rather than interpreting numbers alone, readers can quickly understand where a
movie fits within the JFT library.

The classification should always reflect the movie's overall technical
presentation and not its story, acting, popularity, or cultural significance.

===============================================================================

9.3 OFFICIAL CLASSIFICATIONS

Reference Presentation

Reserved for the finest home theater experiences currently available.

These presentations demonstrate exceptional picture quality, outstanding HDR,
reference-quality Dolby Atmos, remarkable bass performance, and consistently
immersive presentation.

Extremely rare.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Showcase Presentation

An exceptional technical presentation that consistently impresses and serves as
excellent demonstration material.

Minor shortcomings may exist but do not significantly diminish the experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Outstanding Presentation

A technically impressive presentation that excels in most areas while exhibiting
only modest limitations.

Highly recommended for home theater enthusiasts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Excellent Presentation

A consistently strong presentation that fully benefits from modern home theater
equipment.

Minor technical compromises prevent it from reaching the highest tiers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Very Good Presentation

Above-average technical quality with noticeable strengths.

While not reference material, these movies provide an enjoyable and engaging
home theater experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Good Presentation

Competent technical presentation that performs well without standing out.

Suitable for casual viewing but unlikely to be selected as demonstration
material.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Average Presentation

Technically adequate but largely unremarkable.

Presentation neither significantly enhances nor detracts from the viewing
experience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Limited Presentation

Noticeable technical shortcomings reduce the effectiveness of the home theater
experience.

Examples may include weak HDR, limited surround activity, poor transfers,
compressed audio, or dated mastering.

===============================================================================

9.4 CLASSIFICATION CONSISTENCY

Presentation Classifications should remain stable over time.

Changes should occur only when:

• The JFT Score changes.

• A superior transfer becomes available.

• A remastered audio presentation is released.

• Significant recalibration of the JFT Reference Library occurs.

===============================================================================

9.5 REVIEWER REMINDER

The classification should summarize the presentation—not influence the score.

Assign the JFT Score first.

Then determine which Presentation Classification best represents that score.

PART X
DEMO SCENE STANDARDS
===============================================================================

10.1 PURPOSE

The Demo Scene section identifies moments within a movie that best demonstrate
the capabilities of the Jockisch Family Theater.

These scenes showcase exceptional technical presentation and serve as practical
reference material for home theater demonstrations.

Demo scenes should highlight the presentation—not the story.

===============================================================================

10.2 PHILOSOPHY

A demo scene should answer one question:

"If someone wanted to experience what this theater is capable of, what scene
would you play?"

A demo scene may demonstrate:

• Outstanding HDR

• Exceptional Dolby Atmos

• Powerful bass

• Outstanding picture quality

• Surround immersion

• Dynamic range

• A combination of multiple technical strengths

The purpose is to showcase the theater—not summarize the movie.

===============================================================================

10.3 SELECTION CRITERIA

A scene should be included only if it demonstrates a clear technical strength.

Consider:

• Visual impact

• Audio immersion

• Dynamic range

• Bass performance

• Overall presentation

Not every exciting scene qualifies as a demo scene.

Technical excellence—not action—is the determining factor.

===============================================================================

10.4 NUMBER OF DEMO SCENES

Quality is more important than quantity.

Most movies should include between three and six demo scenes.

Only the strongest technical sequences should be selected.

Avoid listing every memorable moment.

===============================================================================

10.5 SCENE DESCRIPTIONS

Descriptions should remain concise.

Explain why the scene is noteworthy without revealing important plot details.

Preferred example:

"An early desert sequence demonstrates exceptional HDR, expansive Atmos
effects, and deep bass."

Avoid:

"The final battle after..."

Protect the viewing experience whenever possible.

===============================================================================

10.6 TIMESTAMPS

Whenever possible, include an approximate timestamp.

Example:

00:18:40

Timestamps allow viewers to quickly locate demonstration material.

Minor variations between editions are acceptable.

===============================================================================

10.7 DEMO CATEGORIES

Each demo scene should identify its primary technical strengths.

Suggested categories include:

HDR

Picture Quality

Dolby Atmos

Surround Immersion

Bass

Dynamic Range

Color

Contrast

Overall Showcase

Multiple categories may be assigned when appropriate.

===============================================================================

10.8 SPOILER POLICY

Demo scenes should remain largely spoiler-free.

Avoid revealing:

Major plot twists

Character deaths

Ending sequences

Unexpected story developments

When an excellent demo scene occurs late in the movie, describe the technical
presentation without explaining its narrative significance.

===============================================================================

10.9 REFERENCE DEMO SCENES

Some scenes may become permanent JFT reference demonstrations.

These scenes are repeatedly used to evaluate:

• New equipment

• Calibration adjustments

• Theater upgrades

• Comparisons between formats

Reference demo scenes should represent the highest technical standards currently
available.

===============================================================================

10.10 REVIEWER REMINDER

Demo scenes exist to demonstrate technical excellence.

They should answer the question:

"What scene best showcases the capabilities of the Jockisch Family Theater?"

Every selected scene should earn its place through exceptional presentation.

• Return output that is immediately ready for use within the Collection App.
